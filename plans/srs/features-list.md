# ResearchNest feature list

1. **Account and roles:** sign up with email verification; log in and out; password reset; settings (notifications, delete account); one account type with roles: Student, Professor, Lab member, Trusted editor, Admin.
2. **Student profile:** research interests, education, papers, target countries and degree.
3. **Search:** plain-language research search; results for professors, labs, universities, papers, positions; "why this matched"; filters (country, university, recruiting, degree); shortlist.
4. **Professor profile:** name, university, topics, recent papers and citations, lab, recruiting status, reply statistics, source label per field, last-updated date.
5. **Lab page:** description, members, research areas, recruiting status, funding note, Q&A, last-updated date.
6. **University page:** name, country, website, associated labs and professors.
7. **Automatic data:** professors, labs, universities and papers imported from OpenAlex and refreshed regularly.
8. **Community editing:** suggest edits with a required source link; approval unless trusted editor or owner; edit history with rollback.
9. **Approval and moderation:** claimed profiles approved by the professor, unclaimed by admins; professors cannot hide statistics or delete answers; review queue; trusted editor after 10 reviewed edits with 90% approval; content hidden after 3 reports.
10. **Profile claiming:** verification with university email; verified badge; owner edits take priority.
11. **Lab Q&A:** questions; verified lab-member answers first; upvotes.
12. **Application tracker:** contacted professors with status; private notes; deadlines and tasks; anonymous reply statistics after at least 5 reports.
13. **Posts and social:** position posts (fixed format, closed after deadline) and lab news by verified users; student "seeking" posts; follow; explore feed; direct messages with block and report.
14. **Notifications:** matching positions, followed updates, answers, edit decisions, deadline reminders, new messages.
